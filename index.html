WebXR Flashlight Starter + Gorilla Locomotion
VR: push off the ground, trees, rocks, tents and the cabin with your hands to move, jump and climb (the mossy log is slippery).
A (right controller) toggles your head-mounted flashlight (it shines wherever you look; it's off in the lobby).
Your hands curl into fists with Grip or Trigger. Cabin doors: grab the handle with Grip or Trigger, or push them open; on desktop press E or click a door. Hit a window hard (or throw a part at it) to smash it, then climb through.
Health: 100 HP on your left wrist. Fires burn you; stay under water longer than about 9 s and you start to drown.
Right thumbstick left/right: 45° snap turn.
Desktop: drag to orbit, F toggles the flashlight, E opens / closes the nearest door (no locomotion).
Generator: hold Trigger or Grip on its yellow handle, then hit each skill check with the other Trigger (or A / X). Beat 5 to power the floodlights.
Four generators are spread round the bigger campground (camp, cabin row, deep woods, lakeside ridge): power all 4 to light the whole camp.
Lake (east of camp): walk down the sandy beach into the deep water, or out along the dock. You slowly sink in the water: swim by sweeping your hands through it to rise and move. Find the propeller and spark plug (hidden far out: a far cabin and behind a rock in the woods) and drop them at the boat's motor; find the jerrycan in another cabin and tilt it over the fuel tank's cap to pour until the gauge reads 100%. Then grab the tiller on the outboard and swing it to steer; Trigger or the thumbstick is the throttle (desktop: arrow keys). Dropped and thrown items bounce, settle and float.
Ambient horror music starts when you press PLAY in the lobby (headphones recommended).
Click anywhere to hear the music  ·  Click PLAY (or press Enter) to start  ·  ENTER VR to play in the headset
