WebXR Flashlight Starter + Gorilla Locomotion
VR: push off the ground, trees, rocks, tents and the cabin with your hands to move, jump and climb (the mossy log is slippery).
A (right controller) toggles your head-mounted flashlight (it shines wherever you look; it's off in the lobby).
Your hands curl into fists with Grip or Trigger. Cabin doors: grab the handle with Grip or Trigger, or push them open; on desktop press E or click a door. Hit a window hard (or throw a part at it) to smash it, then climb through.
Health: 100 HP on your left wrist. Fires burn you; stay under water longer than about 9 s and you start to drown.
Right thumbstick left/right: 45° snap turn.
Desktop: drag to orbit, F toggles the flashlight, E opens / closes the nearest door (no locomotion).
Generator: hold Trigger or Grip on its yellow handle, then hit each skill check with the other Trigger (or A / X). Beat 5 to power the floodlights.
Four generators are spread round the bigger campground (camp, cabin row, deep woods, lakeside ridge): power all 4 to light the whole camp.
Lake (east of camp): walk down the sandy beach into the deep water, or out along the dock. You float high with your head and chest out, and only sink very slowly if you stop swimming: pull your hands back through the water to glide the way you're looking (sweep down to rise, up to dive). Find the propeller and spark plug (the propeller glints on the workbench by the dock; the spark plug is in a far cabin) and drop them at the boat's motor; find the jerrycan in another cabin and tilt it over the fuel tank's cap to pour until the gauge reads 100%. Then grab the tiller on the outboard and swing it to steer; Trigger or the thumbstick is the throttle (desktop: arrow keys). Dropped and thrown items bounce, settle and float.
After PLAY you wait in the waiting room: pick a map on the big screen, watch the MONSTER POV monitor, or spend coins at THE TRADER (shop corner). B / Y (Tab / Esc on desktop) opens the menu: turning and SURVIVOR / MONSTER.
Escape a map (all its generators and objectives done, then the boat) for a sunrise and coins. As the MONSTER in VR, swing the machete hard to hack doors, crates, barricades and trees, and slice branches off.
Ambient horror music starts when you press PLAY in the lobby (headphones recommended).
